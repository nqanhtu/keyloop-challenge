test_seams:
  - Error-code seam: frontend business/error behavior branches on the stable ApiError.code value (never on message text), and expected business rejections such as VEHICLE_NOT_AGING are handled as contained regional outcomes rather than global failures or crashes.
  - Regional containment seam: a `GET /vehicles` or detail failure is contained to the smallest reasonable region with a retry affordance, and the unaffected regions remain usable.

  - Cross-feature coherence seam: after a successful action create and after a rollback, the list rows, detail current action, detail history, and inventory summary stay coherent (server-state views re-read through TanStack Query).
  - Instrumentation seam: a client instrumentation boundary reports client errors and Web Vitals to an injectable sink, and free-text action notes are never included in the reported/telemetry payloads.
